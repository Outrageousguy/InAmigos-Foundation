Who We Are

A family united by the joy of giving

We started InAmigos Foundation in September 2020 with a simple belief — that every person, no matter where they come from, deserves dignity, care, and opportunity. What began as a small circle of compassionate individuals in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, has grown into a movement of over 200 volunteers working across India.

Our founder, Mr. Govind Shukla, built InAmigos on the idea that real change happens when ordinary people come together for extraordinary causes. Today, from feeding the hungry to educating children, from empowering women to planting trees, we are present wherever there is need — and wherever there is hope.

We are a Section 8 registered non-profit, licensed by the Central Government, and operate with full transparency. Every rupee entrusted to us goes toward the people who need it most. Our certifications — 80G, 12A, CSR-1, and NITI Aayog — reflect our commitment to accountability and long-term impact.

Our Initiatives

Six projects, one heart.

Each initiative was born from listening — to communities, to needs, and to the question: how can we help?

Project What we do Why it matters
Project SEVA
Food & Clothing
 We distribute warm meals and essential clothing to homeless individuals, struggling families, and communities hit by disaster. No one should go to bed hungry or face the cold without care.
Over 50,000 meals and clothing kits shared. Ongoing drives run by dedicated local volunteers across India.
Project Bachpanshala
Education
 Every child deserves a shot at learning. We bring digital literacy, life skills, and school education support to underprivileged children who would otherwise be left behind.
Hundreds of first-generation learners now equipped with digital tools and confidence.
Project Udaan
Women Empowerment
 Udaan means “flight.” We give women the wings to soar — through vocational training, financial literacy workshops, and livelihood support that builds real independence.
Women-led groups growing in Chhattisgarh and neighbouring states. Stories of self-reliance, every day.
Project Prakriti
Environment
 The earth gives us everything — it’s our turn to give back. Through tree plantation drives, clean-up campaigns, and sustainability workshops, we protect what sustains us all.
Plantation drives across Bilaspur. Communities taking pride in a cleaner, greener tomorrow.
Project Jeev
Animal Welfare
 Compassion doesn’t stop at humans. We run regular animal feeding drives and welfare programmes for stray and street animals, because every life deserves kindness.
Monthly feeding drives. Awareness campaigns for humane treatment of animals in urban areas.
Project Vikas
Skill Development
 We believe in the potential of India’s youth. Through internship programmes, professional training, and digital skills development, we turn ambition into employability.
30,000+ interns trained nationwide. Our largest programme, actively shaping careers across cities.
